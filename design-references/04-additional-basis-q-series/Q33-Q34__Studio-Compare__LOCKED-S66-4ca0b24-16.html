Claude's recommendation B. Decreasing line + dashed forward-policy overlay. Two reasons: (1) two narratives in one chart — the burn-off curve answers "what do we have?" while the forward-policy line answers "what's the policy?" — and the crossover moment is the institutional read; (2) Q33↔Q34 coupling — burn-off and forward-policy editor are paired surfaces, and B is the only treatment that surfaces the relationship between them at first paint. A's stacked-bar composition is a clean v1.1 enhancement once floorplan-specific concession analysis becomes a real workflow; C's Gantt is the right pattern for ≤30-concession portfolios but breaks at portfolio scale.

Honest read on A and C: A is the cleanest single-chart treatment if floorplan-mix matters as much as total $ — but the rapid-burn months collapse the floorplan stripes to noise. 12-month schedule — % concession varies month-to-month. Captures lease-up tapering: aggressive M1-3 (50%), standard M4-12 (25%). Benefit: only treatment that reflects the time-varying nature of real concession policy. Cost: 12× the input work; most v1 users don't have a 12-month policy thesis yet — they have a single concession assumption.
Claude's recommendation A in v1; B in v1.1. v1 keeps the assumption surface terse — two global inputs is enough for most underwriting workflows, and the simplicity makes the policy cheap to flex against the burn-off line in Q33.B. v1.1 adds floorplan granularity (B) once the user demonstrates the workflow needs it (signal: editing the global value repeatedly, or adding floorplan-specific notes). C's monthly schedule is overkill for v1 — the lease-up taper is a stabilized-property edge case; Studio's 12-month assumption editor handles it via separate scenarios.

Honest read on B: B is technically more accurate but creates a 4× input burden on the v1 user who hasn't yet built a per-floorplan policy thesis. v1.1 promotion timing depends on whether floorplan-mix matters in the burn-off chart (Q33.A win) — if Q33 = A, Q34 = B becomes more compelling; if Q33 = B, Q34 = A holds longer.

v1.1 promotion (per-floorplan inputs) is telemetry-gated: v1 users overriding the global concession amount during scenario authoring at material rate.
