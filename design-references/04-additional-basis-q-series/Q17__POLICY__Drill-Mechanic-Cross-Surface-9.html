  • ThresholdN = 15 rows. Above this, footer becomes arrow-to-slide-out. Anchored against Lease Rollover's 12-month + sub-bucket case (12-18 rows) — covers the typical case cleanly while leaving headroom above Q16 B's 9-row demonstrated case.

  • Footer copyAlways View all N units →. Count required, arrow required. Never View all →, View more, or Show 312 →. The count is the scan-aid that justifies the click.
