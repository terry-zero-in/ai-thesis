Definition
The dollar increase in asset value from T=0 (today's in-place rent capitalized) to M12 (forecast NRI capitalized at the same cap rate). The valuation method controls which forecasted period is annualized in the numerator.
// Value @ T=0
in_place_revenue = in_place_rent × occupied × 12
value_T0 = in_place_revenue ÷ cap_rate

// Value @ M12 · T3 method
nri_T3 = avg(NRI[M10..M12]) × 12
value_M12 = nri_T3 ÷ cap_rate

uplift = value_M12 − value_T0
